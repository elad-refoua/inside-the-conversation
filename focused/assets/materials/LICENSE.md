# Material sources

White Oak Veneer by Poly Haven, downloaded 7 September 2026.

- Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/white_oak_veneer
- Metadata: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/white_oak_veneer
- License: CC0 — https://polyhaven.com/license
- Included: 1K JPEG diffuse/color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps.

The diffuse map is interpreted as sRGB. Normal and roughness maps are linear data. The walnut tone is an in-scene tint; the source files remain unchanged.

Concrete Floor 01 by Poly Haven, downloaded 7 September 2026.

- Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_01
- Metadata: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/concrete_floor_01
- Same CC0 license; included 1K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps.
- The dark stone-like finish is an in-scene tint of this concrete scan.
